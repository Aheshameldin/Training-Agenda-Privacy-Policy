Privacy Policy
Effective date: 20 Sept 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how Training Agenda ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application. We built the App to keep your data on your device wherever possible, and this policy reflects that.
If you have questions about this policy, contact us at: ahmed.heshameldin@gmail.com

1. Information We Collect
1.1 Information Stored Locally on Your Device
The App stores the following information directly on your device (not on our servers):
    • Profile information you enter, such as your name 
    • Workout data, including workout plans you create, exercises, sets, reps, weights, and rest times 
    • Workout history, including completed sessions, logged sets, and performance data 
    • Body weight data, including current weight and target weight, if you choose to enter it 
    • Progress and gamification data, such as experience points, streaks, badges, and completed challenges 
    • Imported workout content, including text or screenshots you choose to import, and any workout data extracted from them 
    • App preferences, such as your preferred weight unit and dismissed in-app notices 
This data is stored locally on your device using standard device storage (SharedPreferences) and is not transmitted to us or to any third party, except as described in Section 1.3 below (subscriptions) or if you explicitly export/share it yourself.
1.2 Camera and Photo Library Access
If you use the "Import a workout" feature, the App may request access to your device's camera or photo library so you can select or capture a screenshot. Any text recognition performed on that image happens entirely on your device using on-device text recognition (Google ML Kit). Images and recognized text are not uploaded to our servers or any third party — they stay on your device unless you choose to save or share them yourself.
1.3 Subscription and Purchase Information
The App offers optional subscriptions. Subscription purchases are processed through Apple's App Store or Google Play, and purchase/entitlement status is managed using RevenueCat, a third-party subscription management service. RevenueCat may receive:
    • A device or user identifier used to track your subscription status 
    • Purchase information (such as which subscription plan you purchased, purchase date, and renewal status) 
RevenueCat does not receive your workout, weight, or personal profile data described in Section 1.1. For more information on how RevenueCat handles data, see their privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
Apple and Google may also independently collect information related to your purchase in accordance with their own privacy policies.
1.4 Information We Do Not Collect
We do not:
    • Require you to create an account or provide an email address to use the App 
    • Collect or sell your personal data to advertisers 
    • Display third-party advertisements in the App 
    • Use your data for behavioral advertising or ad targeting 
    • Have access to your workout, weight, or profile data stored on your device, since it is never transmitted to us 

2. How We Use Information
Because the vast majority of your data stays on your device, we do not "use" it in the traditional sense. The App uses your locally stored data only to:
    • Display your workouts, history, and progress within the App 
    • Calculate coaching recommendations and progressive overload suggestions 
    • Track streaks, badges, and gamification progress 
    • Remember your preferences between app sessions 
Purchase-related information sent to RevenueCat is used solely to verify and manage your subscription status.

3. Data Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Information may only be shared with:
    • RevenueCat, for the purpose of processing and verifying subscription purchases (see Section 1.3) 
    • Apple/Google, as required to process in-app purchases through their platforms 
    • Law enforcement or regulators, only if required by law 

4. Data Retention and Deletion
Your workout, weight, and profile data is stored locally on your device for as long as the App is installed. You can delete this data at any time by:
    • Clearing individual workouts or history within the App, or 
    • Uninstalling the App, which removes all locally stored data from your device 
Since we do not store your personal workout data on our own servers, we have no copy to retain or delete on our end. Subscription records held by RevenueCat, Apple, or Google are retained according to their own respective policies.

5. Children's Privacy
The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can address it.

6. Your Rights
Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete it. Because your workout and profile data is stored locally on your device, you have direct control over it at all times through the App's settings and by uninstalling the App. For questions about data held by RevenueCat, Apple, or Google, please refer to their respective privacy policies or contact them directly.

7. Security
We take reasonable measures to protect information processed in connection with the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this same location with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
